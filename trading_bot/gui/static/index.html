Research-grade validation of the strategy on the data selected under Run configuration (synthetic bars or the CSV history): rolling walk-forward with a refit per window, fractional-vs-baseline ablation, baselines, cost / timing / parameter stress, sanity and leakage tests, bootstrap and Monte Carlo, reproducibility, acceptance gates. Nothing is sent to Alpaca.
Opening the holdout is recorded in holdout_access.jsonl. Do it once, after development is finished; every further opening is flagged.
